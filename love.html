SISTERHOOD

Amanda Igbruke

Sister

When I think about Uyoyo, one of the first things that comes to mind is how determined she’s always been. Even as a child, she knew what she wanted, and once she made up her mind about something, there was very little that could stop her. She’d figure out what she needed to do and just get on with it. That’s something I’ve always admired about her. Growing up with Uyoyo was interesting, to say the least. We were close, as sisters are, but we also argued a lot. And I mean a lot. There was a time when I genuinely wondered why we clashed so much. But I think growing up has a funny way of changing how you see people. Over time, I got to understand her better, and things that I probably didn’t appreciate about her before became things I really love about her now. For as long as I can remember, Uyoyo has been the stylish one in the family. She was always the one who could put an outfit together without even trying. She’s naturally creative, very artsy, and she’s always had a way of making whatever she does her own. And the thing is, she’s actually good at so many things. Once she takes an interest in something, she puts her all into it. She’s also someone who feels things very deeply. I think she’s probably the most emotional out of all of us siblings, and I’ve come to see that as one of her strengths. There’s something really lovely about someone who cares as much as she does. But beyond all of that, what stands out to me the most is her resilience. I’ve watched her deal with difficult situations, things that could easily discourage anyone, and somehow she always finds a way to carry on. She doesn’t let her circumstances define her, and she doesn’t lose herself trying to get through them. I think that takes a lot of strength. And when I think about what I want for her, it’s really quite simple. I want her to be happy. I want her to achieve all the things she’s been working towards, even the ones that might seem a little out of reach right now. I want life to be kind to her, and I hope she never loses that determination that has always been such a big part of who she is. I’ve seen how much she wants out of life, and more than anything, I just want to be there to watch it all come together for her.
